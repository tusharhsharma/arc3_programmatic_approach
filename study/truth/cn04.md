## Controls (exactly what each available action does)
- No available_actions argument (cn04.py:846) -> engine default; the game handles ACTION1-6 only.
- Exactly one piece is "selected" (`xseexqzst`); it is drawn on layer 4 with its body recoloured to black 0 (connectors keep 8, matched keep 3) (cn04.py:888-904). Initially the selected piece is the visible sprite minimising x^2+y^2 (cn04.py:881-884).
- ACTION1/2/3/4 = move the selected piece by (0,-1)/(0,1)/(-1,0)/(1,0) one grid cell, only if it stays inside the 20x20 grid (cn04.py:1108-1125). No collision: pieces can overlap freely.
- ACTION5 = rotate the selected piece +90 deg (engine rotate() adds delta; render rotates clockwise) about its top-left anchor, then clamp back into the grid (cn04.py:1093-1096, 1174-1182). Exception: if the selected piece belongs to a STACK (several sprites created at the same (x,y), cn04.py:872-880), ACTION5 instead cycles which sprite of the stack is visible (ping-pong order) at the same position (cn04.py:1091-1093, 1133-1157) — L5 and L6 only.
- ACTION6 click (display coords): converted with display_to_grid (cn04.py:1065); the first VISIBLE sprite whose rendered non-transparent pixel is at that cell becomes selected (cn04.py:1069-1073, 1087, 1159-1172). Clicking the already-selected piece: if it is in a stack and the clicked pixel is body colour 0, cycle the stack (cn04.py:1075-1081); otherwise deselect it (1083,1085). Clicking empty space does nothing.
- Every action costs 1 step (engine _action_count); moves against the wall still cost.

## Mechanics a first-time human must discover, in the order a human would (L1)
1. The black piece is the one under control; ACTION1-4 slide it one cell; it stops at the grid edge (cn04.py:1108-1125).
2. ACTION5 turns the black piece 90 degrees about its top-left corner (cn04.py:1095).
3. Clicking a coloured piece makes it the black (controlled) one; the old one regains its colour (cn04.py:1087, 888-918).
4. Pieces pass through each other; when a grey connector pixel of one piece lands exactly on a grey connector pixel of another piece, both turn green 3 (cn04.py:989-1018).
5. Hidden distinction: there are two connector kinds (8 and 13) drawn identically; a pair turns green for display even for mixed kinds (cn04.py:989-994 uses the combined list) but only same-kind pairs count for the win (977-988, 1023-1049). Therefore a green pairing that does not end the level must be the wrong kind — swap the orientation.
6. The level ends when every connector on every piece is green (cn04.py:1047-1049).
