## Mechanics a first-time human must discover, in the order a human would (L1)
1. ACTION1-4 slide the grey shape (the one with black centre dots) one cell; it stops at the grid edge (ar25.py:1731-1758).
2. A yellow copy of the shape sits mirrored across the vertical blue line; moving the shape moves the yellow copy the opposite way in x, same in y (ar25.py:1579-1599, 1682-1686).
3. The blue dots form an L that is the mirror image of the grey L; the level ends when the yellow mirror image lies exactly on the dots (ar25.py:1688-1696).
4. Energy bar (right column) shrinks with each move; ACTION7 undoes a move for free (1716-1721).
5. (L2+) ACTION5/click switch control to the blue axis line, which slides sideways and drags the mirror image with it (1779-1791, 1740-1743).
6. (L5+) Two axes: images of images also count (depth-12 iteration, 1579-1599).
