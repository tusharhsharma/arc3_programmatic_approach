## Controls (exactly what each available action does)
- available_actions=[1,2,3,4,6] (sc25.py:1764).
- ACTION1/2/3/4: sets the facing (used by fireball, 2696-2707), then try_move by 2 px (small) or 4 px (big; if 4 is blocked it retries 2 px) (2691, 2713-2718). Blocked by any collidable sprite (walls, barriers, crystals, sprite-21; base_game.py:366-400). Walking onto an ENERGY sprite deletes it and refunds 10 steps (2721-2730). Colliding with the DOOR triggers the win (2731-2747). Each move costs 1 step (2748, 1890-1898).
- ACTION6 on a grid cell or its click pad: toggles that cell; costs 1 step; if the 3x3 pattern now equals an allowed spell the cast starts inside the same action (2647-2672, 2431-2438). ACTION6 on the spell book / a spell icon: selects that spell as the HINT (its cells are drawn black in the grid); clicking the already-selected icon replays a demo lighting its cells one by one; free (2613-2646). ACTION6 anywhere else: the grid flashes colour 8 for 12 frames; free (2673-2680, 2332-2371).
- Cast resolution (2253-2267): TELEPORT moves the player onto the next pad of its size class, cycling (2186-2251); SIZE toggles scale 1<->2, growing only if a 4x4 spot exists within offsets -2..0 (2111-2153, 2496-2532), otherwise a red shake and no change; FIREBALL fires a bolt in the facing direction that flies until the first cell of a wall/barrier/crystal (1967-2028), and if that cell belongs to `tagsmh` the crystal and every `dosorb` vanish (2083-2093), for `seofsw-tagsmh` every `seofsw-dosorb` vanishes (2094-2102). After any cast the grid is cleared and 1 more step is charged (2576-2601). Growing onto ENERGY also collects it (2317-2326).

## Mechanics a first-time human must discover, in the order a human would (L1)
1. The first key press does nothing but light up four grid cells one after another (the demo, 2542-2555); afterwards arrows move the big square 4 px along the corridor (2713).
2. The corridor narrows to 2 px at x<23 and the 4x4 player is blocked there (try_move); the grid shows four black cells in a diamond (hint, 2440-2461).
3. Clicking a grid cell toggles it (colour 14) and costs a step; completing the diamond turns the cells colour 15, the player flashes, and the player shrinks to 2x2 (2647-2672, 2253-2265, 2111-2153).
4. The small player moves 2 px per press and fits through the gap; touching the door frame ends the level (2731-2747).
5. (L2+) Other patterns: XX./.X. teleports to the ringed pad; .X./.X./.X. shoots a bolt in the last-moved direction; a bolt hitting a crystal removes the matching barriers (2083-2102); walking onto a colour-14 dot refunds 10 steps.
