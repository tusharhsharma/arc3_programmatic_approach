## Controls (exactly what each available action does)
- available_actions=[6] (s5i5.py:2042). Every ACTION6 decrements the step counter first (2189), whatever it hits.
- Click on a SLIDER of colour C (2219-2242): let m = half the slider's long dimension and u = click offset along that dimension. If u > m (bottom half of a vertical slider / right half of a horizontal one): every ARM of colour C grows by one 3-px unit away from its base (2235-2236, 2110-2136: orientation 0 grows upward and the arm's y shifts up; 90 grows rightward; 180 downward; 270 leftward with x shift). If u < m and the arm has >1 unit: shrinks by one unit (2237-2238). Clicking exactly on the middle bar does nothing. Children (tips, child arms, their descendants) are translated by the same delta (2135-2136, 2088-2092).
- Click on a ROTATE BUTTON with centre colour C (2195-2213): every ARM of colour C is rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise about its base end (2154-2179, np.rot90) together with its whole child subtree (2145-2152). If the arm is itself a child and the rotation would leave it pointing straight back along its parent (|child_rot-90-parent_rot|==180), it rotates twice instead (2204-2211).
- After either change, if any ARM overlaps any other ARM (walls, beams, chains included; pixel-perfect on non -1 pixels; 2138-2143) the whole change is reverted on the next tick (2094-2098 snapshot, 2182-2187 restore, 2214-2215/2239-2240). The step is still spent.
